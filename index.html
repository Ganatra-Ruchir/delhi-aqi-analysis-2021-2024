Research notes & data quality

This explorer uses the original CSV checked into this repository. Its upstream station coverage, collection methodology, concentration units and revision history are not fully documented. Pollutant values are displayed in source units; they should not be mixed or compared as equivalent concentrations. This historical dataset is not a live air-quality service.

Inspect quality checks and method

Means exclude missing numeric cells. Counts, maximums, category totals and consecutive-day runs are calculated from the current filter. No causal conclusions, clinical recommendations or predictive accuracy claims are made.
